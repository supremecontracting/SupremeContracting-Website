When Do You Need To Repair Your Roof?

Before you start down the path of investing in a new roof, let the pros at Supreme Contracting take a look. It's very possible that our roof inspection would determine that your current roof could be just fine with a few minor repairs.

But what are the signs that indicate you might need to hire the Supreme Team to fix your roof? Well, here are a few to watch out for:

  • Roof shingles are frayed, cracked, or broken.

  • Roof shingles are curled or pulling away.

  • Roof shingles are missing.

  • Metal roof shingles or panels are rusting.

  • Asphalt shingles show bald areas.

  • Leaking is evident.

  • Insulation is wet or ruined.

  • Mold, moss, mildew, or algae are growing in the attic, ceilings, or walls.

  • It's difficult to regulate the temperature inside the house.

  • Energy costs are going up (meaning energy efficiency is going down).

With the elements of weather and the march of time, some damage to your roof is inevitable. If you observe any of the above indicators on your Northern Virginia home, it's time to call Supreme Contracting to do an assessment of your roof's condition and provide guidance about roof repair or roof replacement.

Every roof has a lifespan, the length of which is determined by the durability of the roofing materials and the quality of the installation. Roofs composed of asphalt shingles or wood shakes tend to last between 10 and 20 years; slate and metal roofs can go for 50 years or more, if installed right and maintained well.

Maximize the useful life of your roof with occasional inspections and as-needed repairs by the experts at Supreme Contracting in Northern Virginia.

Common Roof Problems that Supreme Contracting Can Repair

Roofs can get damaged all sorts of ways. Obviously there's the impact of large hailstones, high winds, and other manifestations of extreme weather. There's also the impact of falling branches and limbs, and the occasional well-hit baseball.

Sometimes critters like birds and squirrels set up homes on your roof, which can possibly led to broken or missing shingles. And of course there's the inexorable passage of Father Time, which causes everything to eventually break down.

Whatever the cause of the roof damage, Supreme Contracting offers the solution.

Here are some typical roofing issues that the Supreme Team can cure with our roof repair services:

  • blistering

  • sagging (causing water to puddle or pond)

  • punctures and holes

  • leaking

  • damaged flashing

  • damaged, curled, or uplifted shingles

  • missing shingles

  • poor ventilation

Don't delay taking care of your roofing needs. Bigger problems can arise if issues like punctures, leaks, and missing shingles aren't addressed. Let Supreme Contracting keep your roof in good shape - just as we've done for many Northern VA residential homes over the years.
