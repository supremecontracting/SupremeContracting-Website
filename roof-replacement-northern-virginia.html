Roof Leak Repair, Shingle Repair, Flashing Fixes & More

Complete Residential Roof Replacement Services Across NoVA

Your roof system is more than shingles. Leaks often start at flashing, vents, skylights, chimneys, valleys, or failing sealants.

We repair and restore the most common roofing problems for Northern Virginia homeowners:

  • Roof leak detection & repair (including attic inspections and water tracing)

  • Missing, lifted, or damaged shingles (wind damage, nail pops, granule loss)

  • Flashing repairs (chimneys, walls, step flashing, counter flashing)

  • Pipe boot / vent repairs and seal replacements

  • Storm damage repairs (hail impacts, tree limb damage, wind-driven rain)

  • Flat roof membrane repairs (when applicable)

  • Gutter and drainage corrections to prevent future water intrusion

Whether your home has asphalt shingles, architectural shingles, metal panels, or a flat roofing section, we’ll provide a repair plan that restores performance and extends roof life.
